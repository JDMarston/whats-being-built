# Sources

Use public records first. Local reporting and developer pages are useful for filling in details and checking status.

## Public records

- City of St. Petersburg development, permit, zoning, meeting, and agenda pages
- Pinellas County property and aerial imagery services
- Hillsborough County aerial imagery for Tampa-area coverage
- Other city or county open-data feeds as new areas are added

## Other sources

- Developer project pages
- St Pete Rising
- St. Pete Catalyst
- Florida YIMBY
- Local reporting that names the project and location

## Rules

- Save the project facts and the link, not the full article.
- Do not scrape pages behind a login or other access controls.
- Keep imports in the review queue until the address and coordinates are believable.
- Remove completed projects two years after completion.
